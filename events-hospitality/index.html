Frequently Asked Questions

01Can drones be used indoors for event coverage?

Yes, SKYi’s drones can be used indoors for event coverage, provided that the venue’s layout and regulations allow for safe drone operations.

02Can I request specific shots or angles for my event coverage?

Absolutely! SKYi accommodates specific requests for shots or angles to ensure that your event coverage captures the key moments and perspectives that are important to you.

03How long does it take to receive the edited footage and photography after an event?

The timeframe for receiving edited footage and photography depends on factors such as the duration of the event and any specific editing requests. However, we strive to deliver the final content promptly to meet your needs.

04Can I use the aerial footage and photography for marketing purposes?

Yes, the aerial footage and photography captured by SKYi’s drones are perfect for marketing purposes, including promotional materials, social media, websites, and advertising campaigns.

05Are your drone pilots licensed and insured?

Yes, all of SKYi’s drone pilots are licensed and insured, ensuring compliance with regulations and safety standards for aerial operations.

06How do I get started with scheduling Drone Services for Hospitality with SKYi?

Simply contact us to discuss your event coverage and venue showcasing needs, and we’ll work with you to customise a drone service package that meets your requirements and exceeds your expectations.
