01How long does it take to capture a time-lapse sequence?

The duration of the time-lapse sequence depends on the specific requirements of your project. We'll work closely with you to establish a timeframe that meets your needs.

Yes, SKYi provides customisable options for interval timing, allowing you to tailor the time-lapse sequence to your preferences.

Our team plans flights with minimal disruption, prioritising safety and adherence to regulations. We ensure that our operations integrate seamlessly with your project.
