Frequently Asked Questions

Can't see your question here? Our team is happy to help — just get in touch.

01Can drones be used for property inspections in all weather conditions?

SKYi’s drones are equipped to operate in a variety of weather conditions, including light rain and moderate wind. However, severe weather conditions may impact drone operations and require rescheduling of inspections.

02How far in advance should I schedule property drone services?

It is recommended to schedule property drone services at least a few weeks in advance to ensure availability and accommodate any specific requirements or preferences.

03Can I request specific areas of focus for property inspections?

Absolutely! SKYi tailors property drone services to meet your specific needs and priorities. You can discuss your requirements with our team, and we’ll ensure that the inspection covers the areas of interest.

04Are your drone operations compliant with regulations?

Yes, SKYi adheres to all regulations and guidelines governing drone operations, ensuring compliance and safety at all times.

05How quickly can I receive inspection reports after a property drone service?

SKYi strives to deliver inspection reports promptly following the completion of drone services. The timeframe may vary depending on the scope of the service and the level of analysis required.

06Can property drone services be customised for commercial and residential properties?

Yes, SKYi offers property drone services tailored to both commercial and residential properties, providing customised solutions to meet the unique needs of each property type.
