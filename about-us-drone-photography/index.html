Our Vision

Our vision is to revolutionise visual storytelling by elevating perspectives and capturing moments from the sky that resonate with emotions and create lasting memories.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, reliable, and fully insured drone photography and videography services that exceed our clients’ expectations. We strive to consistently deliver breathtaking aerial footage and photographs that bring our clients’ visions to life.

Our Process

At SKYi Drone Photography, we have a streamlined process to ensure that your aerial photography and videography needs are met with efficiency and precision. From the initial consultation to the final touches, we work with you every step of the way to ensure that your vision is brought to life.

  1. Pre-flight Planning

    Our team of experts will work with you to plan out every detail of your aerial shoot, including flight plans, equipment needs, and safety precautions.

  2. On-Site Drone Management

    Our team of experienced pilots and photographers will execute your aerial shoot with precision and attention to detail.

  3. Photography and Videography

    Using state-of-the-art drones and equipment, we capture stunning aerial footage and images for your project or event.

  4. Post-Production

    We use cutting-edge software and technology to edit and enhance your aerial footage and images, ensuring that the final product is of the highest quality.
